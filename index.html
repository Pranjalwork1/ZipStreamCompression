Frequently Asked Questions About PDF Compression

How do I compress a PDF online for free?

Select or drop your PDF into ZipStream at zipstream.online/compress-pdf, choose your compression profile, and click Compress. Your reduced PDF downloads immediately.

Does ZipStream upload my files to a server?

No. ZipStream executes all compression and formatting directly in your browser using WebAssembly. Your files stay on your device and are never sent to external cloud storage.

Can I compress PDF without losing quality?

Yes. Balanced compression removes redundant font subsets, stream metadata, and optimizes imagery without causing noticeable visual degradation to document text or graphics.
